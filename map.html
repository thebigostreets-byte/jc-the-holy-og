What is measured and what is generated?

Source building shapes and roof elevations: NGA 2014 scene. Horizontal positions: WGS84 / UTM zone 11N. Scene vertical frame: EGM96. The source can split one property into several building features.

Roof and ground appearance: June 2022 USGS/USDA aerial images. Wall appearance: generated photographic materials and assigned paint colors, not a surveyed record of each property.

Terrain is interpolated from building bases. Separate 2022 LiDAR sample is included in the full pack without merging its unspecified vertical datum. The editor shows source roads in imagery. Play mode adds a navigable Las Vegas road overlay, side streets, an overpass, traffic and landmark models.
