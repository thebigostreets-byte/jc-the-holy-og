Abilities, skating & controller

Press Tab or tap POWERS on the right joystick to open the translucent ability wheel. Select from Powers, Grace, Movement or Combat to equip an ability, then press X or the CAST button to use it. Hold CAST for lightning, Holy Beam and block; release to fire or stop. The game pauses while the wheel is open. Escape closes it. On mobile, the left stick moves and the right stick aims. Hold the centre of the right stick to open the wheel, drag to choose, and release to equip. The arrow buttons change categories. EXIT the car to fly; FLY takes off, RISE and DESCEND change altitude, FAST accelerates, BOOST bursts forward, and BRAKE slows you down. All original hotkeys still work. K equips the board. Hold W to push, release to coast, S or B brakes, Shift pushes faster, C toggles hover, Space ollies, and J changes the airborne trick pose. L is currently a grind pose only; rail physics are unfinished.

1 fires Judgment straight down. Hold 2 or 3, then release at 100% for lightning or Holy Beam. 4 lifts an aimed building or vehicle; 5 throws it. 6 casts prayer; moving or taking damage interrupts it. 7 steps along a clear path. 8 slows the pursuer. 9 activates a protective halo. 0 restores a nearby visible lost soul. Mercy Rain in the Grace wheel (comma on keyboard) costs 30 Grace and puts out fires within 110 metres for 12 seconds. JC’s car has a golden halo and a Godspeed boost; hold BRAKE to slow the car.

N holds block; press just before a frontal hit to parry. G calls Glory Storm: lightning strikes up to three nearby visible targets, or fires forward when none are nearby. I locks or unlocks a visible target; [ and ] cycle. U reloads, Y draws or holsters. O/P evade forward/back.

Standard gamepad: left stick moves; right stick looks; A jumps, B casts the equipped ability, X boosts, Y enters the car; LB flies, RB shields; triggers charge powers; Start pauses and resumes. Back/Select opens the wheel. While it is open, either stick selects, LB/RB change category, A equips and B cancels. USB N64 adapters vary and may need the adapter’s mapping utility. Hardware compatibility is not yet verified.
